開始遊戲

  1. 開啟 App，在標題畫面點一下螢幕（左下角的「同 Wi-Fi 對戰」以外的地方）。
  2. 選你的英雄、電腦對手與場地，開打。也可以進訓練場練習，或觀看電腦對戰。
  3. 不需要帳號、不用登入，沒有網路也能玩。

同 Wi-Fi 對戰

  1. 兩支裝置連上同一個 Wi-Fi。
  2. 標題畫面左下角點「同 Wi-Fi 對戰」：一台「開房」，另一台「加入」並選擇房間。
  3. 第一次使用時，iOS 會詢問「區域網路」權限，兩台都要按「允許」。

找不到房間？

  • 到 iOS「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 區域網路」，確認 AI模型大亂鬥 已開啟。
  • 確認兩台在同一個 Wi-Fi，不是一台用行動網路；部分公共或訪客 Wi-Fi 會隔離裝置，請改用家用 Wi-Fi 或手機熱點。
  • 兩台都更新到相同版本的 App。

常見問題

有線上對戰嗎？

1.0 版提供離線對戰電腦與同 Wi-Fi 對戰。網路線上對戰仍在準備中，上線時會在 App Store 更新說明。

需要付費或有內購嗎？

沒有內購、沒有廣告。

遊戲會用到 AI 嗎？

「AI 模型」指的是英雄的設計過程：每位英雄都和 AI 模型協作設計，選角卡上有一枚「AI 設計」印記。遊玩時不會連線任何 AI 服務，也不需要 AI 帳號或金鑰。本作與任何 AI 模型供應商沒有隸屬、贊助或背書關係。

存檔在哪裡？

設定與進度存在你的裝置上；刪除 App 會一併刪除。詳見隱私政策。

Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap the title screen (anywhere except the Same Wi-Fi button at the bottom left).
  2. Pick your hero, a CPU opponent and a stage, then fight. You can also practise in training or watch the CPU fight.
  3. No account or sign-in is needed, and you can play without an internet connection.

Same-Wi-Fi battles

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. On the title screen, tap Same Wi-Fi (bottom left): one device hosts, the other joins and picks the room.
  3. The first time, iOS asks for Local Network permission. Allow it on both devices.

Can’t see the room?

  • Check iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure the game is switched on.
  • Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi, not mobile data. Some public or guest Wi-Fi networks isolate devices; try a home network or a personal hotspot.
  • Update both devices to the same app version.

FAQ

Is there online play?

Version 1.0 offers offline CPU duels and same-Wi-Fi battles. Internet play is still in preparation and will be announced in an App Store update.

Are there purchases or ads?

No in-app purchases and no ads.

Does the game use AI?

“AI models” refers to how the heroes were designed: each hero was designed in collaboration with AI models, and its select card carries a small “AI design” seal. The game never connects to an AI service while you play and needs no AI account or key. The game is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any AI model provider.

Where is my progress saved?

Settings and progress stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app. See the Privacy Policy.
