隱私政策

適用：iPhone App「AI模型大亂鬥」（LARGE LANGUAGE MAYHEM）1.0 版起　·　生效日：2026 年 10 月 22 日

繁體中文English

  • 不需要帳號、不用登入。我們不要求你的姓名、電子郵件、電話、生日或任何身分資料。
  • App 不會把你的資料傳給我們。這個版本沒有自己的伺服器連線：沒有線上大廳、沒有雲端存檔、沒有數據分析。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤，不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA），不販售、不分享任何資料。
  • 遊戲不會在遊玩時呼叫任何 AI 模型；你的遊玩過程也不會被用來訓練 AI。

1. 留在你裝置上的資料

設定（語言、音量、觸控大小與透明度、畫面選項）與遊玩進度只存在你的裝置上，App 用它們來記住你的選擇。我們看不到這些資料。刪除 App 就會一併刪除。

2. 同 Wi-Fi 對戰（區域網路）

「區網對戰」會請你允許 iOS 的「區域網路」權限，用來在同一個 Wi-Fi 上找到並連線另一支裝置。對戰時，兩支裝置之間直接交換遊戲需要的資料：操作輸入、所選英雄、連線狀態，以及房間顯示用的裝置標籤（例如「手機 23」，取自裝置在這個 Wi-Fi 上位址的最後一段數字）。這些資料只在你們兩台裝置之間傳送，不會經過或送到我們的伺服器，對戰結束後也不保留。

你可以拒絕這個權限，離線對戰電腦、訓練場與觀戰都照常可以玩；之後可到 iOS「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 區域網路」重新開啟。

3. Apple 處理的資料

下載、更新與 App Store 帳號由 Apple 處理，適用 Apple 隱私權政策。如果你在 iOS 設定中選擇「與 App 開發者分享」分析資料，Apple 可能提供我們匯總後的當機與使用統計；這些資料不含可以直接識別你的身分的資訊，由 Apple 依其政策收集與處理，你隨時可在「設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 分析與改進項目」關閉。

4. 我們不收集的資料

我們不收集：姓名、電子郵件、電話、地址、付款資訊、精確或大概位置、聯絡人、照片、相機、麥克風、健康資料、瀏覽紀錄、廣告識別碼或裝置序號。App 沒有文字或語音聊天，也沒有內購。

5. 兒童

本 App 不是專為兒童設計，我們也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的資料——事實上 App 不收集任何人的個人資料。家長若有疑問，請用下方方式聯絡我們。

6. 你的權利

因為我們沒有保存你的個人資料，所以沒有需要查詢、更正、匯出或刪除的伺服器資料；裝置上的資料，你可以隨時刪除 App 來清除。你依所在地法律（例如澳洲《1988 年隱私法》、台灣《個人資料保護法》、歐盟 GDPR）享有的權利不受影響；有任何問題都可以聯絡我們。

7. 外部連結

App 內的「隱私政策」按鈕會用 Safari 開啟本頁面。本網站以 GitHub Pages 靜態託管，不使用 Cookie、不載入分析或廣告程式；GitHub 作為主機商可能為了安全與營運記錄存取資訊，適用 GitHub 隱私聲明。

8. 政策變更

如果未來版本加入線上對戰、帳號或其他會傳送資料的功能，我們會在該版本上架前更新本政策與 App Store 的「App 隱私權」說明，並在這裡更新生效日。

Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone app “LARGE LANGUAGE MAYHEM” (AI模型大亂鬥), version 1.0 and later · Effective 22 October 2026

繁體中文English

  • No account, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or any identity data.
  • The app does not send your data to us. This version has no connection to our own servers: no online lobby, no cloud saves, no analytics.
  • No ads and no tracking. No advertising identifier (IDFA); we do not sell or share any data.
  • The game does not call any AI model while you play, and your play is not used to train AI.

1. Data that stays on your device

Your settings (language, volume, touch size and opacity, display options) and your progress are stored only on your device so the app can remember your choices. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Same-Wi-Fi battles (local network)

LAN Battle asks for iOS’s Local Network permission to find and connect to another device on the same Wi-Fi. During a match the two devices exchange what the game needs directly with each other: control inputs, chosen heroes, connection status and a device label for the room list (such as “手機 23”, taken from the last number of the device’s address on that Wi-Fi). This data travels only between your two devices, never through or to our servers, and is not kept after the match.

You can decline the permission: offline CPU duels, training and spectate still work. You can turn it back on in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

3. Data handled by Apple

Downloads, updates and your App Store account are handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. If you choose “Share with App Developers” in iOS analytics settings, Apple may give us aggregated crash and usage statistics that do not directly identify you. Apple collects and processes these under its own policy, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, payment details, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, health data, browsing history, advertising identifiers or device serial numbers. The app has no text or voice chat and no in-app purchases.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children’s data — in fact the app collects no personal data from anyone. Parents with questions can contact us as shown below.

6. Your rights

Because we keep no personal data about you, there is no server-side data to access, correct, export or delete; you can clear the on-device data at any time by deleting the app. Your rights under the laws where you live (for example the Australian Privacy Act 1988, Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act or the EU GDPR) are not affected, and you are welcome to contact us with any question.

7. Links

The in-app “Privacy Policy” button opens this page in Safari. This site is a static GitHub Pages site with no cookies, analytics or ad scripts; as the host, GitHub may log access information for security and operations under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Changes

If a future version adds online play, accounts or any other feature that sends data, we will update this policy and the App Store “App Privacy” details before that version is released, and change the effective date here.
